01 — Briefing

WHAT THEY DIDN'T TELL YOU

Past the containment line the ash is still falling. What moves under it stopped being our problem the day the line closed. It is yours now.

There are 175 sectors. We have maps for most of them. We will not tell you which maps lie.

Every fifth sector is a hold-out. Every tenth, something older wakes up. Sector 175 is not on any map we will give you. It exists. That is all you need to know before you go.
