WHAT THEY DIDN'T TELL YOU

Past the containment line the ash is still falling. What moves under it stopped being our problem the day the line closed. It is yours now.

There are 175 sectors. We have maps for most of them. We will not tell you which maps lie. Every fifth is a hold-out. Every tenth, something older wakes up. Sector 175 is not on any map we will give you. It exists. That is all you need to know before you go.

The entire operation fits in a single .html file. Download it, open it, and it runs — offline, anywhere, forever. Your record travels with you.

DOWNLOADED — 30 SECONDS TO A DESKTOP ICON:
1. Open your Downloads folder and move ASHFALL.html anywhere you like — desktop is perfect.
2. Right-click the file → Send to → Desktop (create shortcut).
3. Double-click the shortcut anytime. It launches fullscreen-style, runs offline, and saves your record on that machine. That icon is the game.
