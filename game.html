SETTINGS
Briefing
MISSION — clear all 175 sectors. Every 10th holds an Alpha hostile. Every 5th is a hold-out — survive 60 seconds until extraction.
WEATHER — some sectors catch rain (−10% speed, −10% vision) or a fog bank (−25% vision). They can stack. Watch the sky report on deploy.
MOVEMENT — WASD / arrows / left stick. On touch, the stick is analog. AIM + FIRE — mouse / right stick.
BUILDINGS — most are enterable. Windows stop bodies — bullets shatter them. Reinforced windows stop bullets too — bring explosives or patience. Fences line the streets and block the unready.
EXPLOSIVES ARE HONEST — barrels, blowouts and rockets don't care who lit them. Your own RPG will hurt you.
SUPPLY DROPS — up to three aid crates per sector. Patch-up health and a plate that's good for that round only.
THE LAPTOP — one per sector (G / PC button). Five damage. Eight-second stun. On anything.
HAZARDS — barrels burst · terminals fry · vents poison · spikes wound · steam scalds · crates stagger.
SIDE CHANNEL — Endless, Sandbox, Bosses Only. None of it touches your campaign record.
HEALTH CARRIES OVER between sectors. Patch up before you push.
